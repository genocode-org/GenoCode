# Community creatures

Creatures shared by people who use GenoCode, from the app's **Share with the community** button.
They appear on the website at <https://genocode.piorek.co.za/community/> and in the app's list of
creatures.

## How a creature gets here

1. Someone shares a creature from the app. No account is needed: they give a name to be credited
   by and a few words about it.
2. It arrives as an issue labelled `community`. A check runs on it and comments a picture of it,
   and anything wrong with its code.
3. A maintainer looks at it and adds the label `approved`, which opens a pull request adding it
   here. GitHub asks a maintainer to approve that pull request's checks ("Approve workflows to
   run"), as it was opened by a bot. Merging it puts the creature on the website.

## What gets approved

- It is a creature, has no errors and builds within GenoCode's limits.
- It is the sharer's own work, or a change of a creature shared under terms that allow it.
- Nothing hateful, sexual, violent or meant to hurt anyone, in its look, its name or its words.
